Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Just Say No mobile application ("the App").

Information We Collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection.

Local Device Storage

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • habit tracking data
  • progress history
  • achievement or reward information
  • app settings or preferences

This data is stored on your device and used only to operate the App.

Platform Diagnostics

Mobile operating systems such as Apple iOS may collect technical diagnostics including:

  • device model
  • operating system version
  • crash reports

This information is handled by the platform provider under their own privacy policies.

How Information Is Used

Information stored by the App is used only to:

  • operate the app
  • display user progress
  • maintain functionality

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Data Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties except when required by law.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information stored by the App, but no digital system can guarantee complete security.

Data Deletion

You may delete all app data by uninstalling the App from your device.

If you would like assistance deleting any information associated with the App, you may contact us using the email below.

Changes to This Policy
